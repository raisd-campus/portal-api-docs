Applicant enrolment confirmation

#

Historical 0.6.1 captures retain the earlier single-confirmation flow. New 0.6.2 v2 captures show QA Eligibility confirmation followed by Registry Offer acceptance, with spaced equal-width icon actions, native PDF preview in a new tab and filename-preserving download. Overall review remains orange until final acceptance; four checks separately track payment, documents, applicant confirmation and Registry Offer release. Applicant owns approved-enrolment acceptance and the development next-login workflow. This composition uses the released 0.6.1 Popup, DetailField and buttons unchanged; confirmation displays intake, faculty and programme and explains automatic logout followed by an explicit Sign in click. Failed logout keeps accepted status and retries only logout. Captures are from an uncommitted Applicant working tree, not a new package release or live authentication.
