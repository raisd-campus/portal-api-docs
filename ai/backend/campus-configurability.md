# Campus configurability and University Flows

Current owner-approved implementation, 20 October 2026. This explicitly supersedes the earlier first-flow hard-coding/hold instruction from the 19 October stand-up.

Control **University Flows → Admissions** manages supported structured rules per campus. Save a draft, validate/preview local/international and new/returning scenarios, then publish an immutable effective version with a reason. Existing cases retain their pinned policy; restoring an older configuration publishes another version. All eight campuses start with the same owner-selected Demo template, not a claim of university/jurisdictional approval.

Menu access and business policy remain separate. Control uses active-role flows.configure; Staff reviews use active Admin campus/desk permissions. Applicant access stays fixed. IO and SSD capabilities are assigned explicitly from People, never by enabling a policy stage. Missing prices, dates, credit decisions and unconfirmed local/returning requirements stay manual. Disabling collection never waives a debt.

See [University Flows](university-flows.md) for confirmed defaults, provenance, operator steps, API/storage boundaries, verification and the prioritised extension inventory. Hosted help remains [campus-configurability.html](../../diagrams/campus-configurability.html). Later flows require their own approved scope; arbitrary scripts and automatic tax/refund formulas are excluded.
