
# LMS / ODL REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST


---

# 1. BOTSWANA

**Main Authority:** Botswana Qualifications Authority (BQA)  
**Regulatory Strength:** Moderate  
**Overall Position:** General accreditation and quality-assurance requirements apply to online/distance provision, but the reviewed material does not establish a dedicated national LMS feature standard.

## Governance & Accreditation

[ ] Verify the institution/provider is appropriately accredited by BQA. — General QA

[ ] Verify the ODL/online programme is accredited. — General QA

[ ] Maintain evidence of institutional and programme readiness for online delivery. — General QA

## Course Design & Content

[ ] Clearly document course information and intended learning outcomes. — General QA

[ ] Provide suitable access to digital learning materials/resources. — General QA

[ ] Maintain a process for reviewing and assuring the quality of learning materials. — General QA

## Assessment

[ ] Maintain an appropriate assessment system for online/ODL programmes. — General QA

## Learner Support

[ ] Provide appropriate learner support services. — General QA

[ ] Provide access to suitable library/e-learning resources. — General QA

[ ] Provide appropriate study-centre or off-site support where applicable. — General QA

## Technology

[ ] Ensure adequate internet/connectivity and supporting infrastructure. — General QA

## Quality & Staff

[ ] Monitor quality and effectiveness of ODL delivery. — General QA

[ ] Ensure staff have appropriate training and competence for programme delivery. — General QA

## External Arrangements

[ ] Check accreditation and quality controls for external, cross-border or third-party arrangements. — General QA

### Items Requiring Further Verification

No explicit national LMS requirement was confirmed for:

- Dedicated LMS platform
- Self-learning materials
- Multimedia requirements
- Copyright controls
- Learner-instructor interaction
- Learner-learner interaction
- Live/synchronous learning
- Asynchronous learning
- Interactive online activities
- Instructor feedback
- Identity verification
- Anti-plagiarism
- Online grades/progress access
- LMS induction
- Dedicated IT helpdesk
- Accessibility requirements
- LMS capacity/reliability monitoring
- Backup/redundancy
- Privacy/data security
- Cybersecurity
- Mobile accessibility
- Learner engagement tracking
- Student ICT skills

**Key Caution:** Treat these as matters requiring BQA verification rather than as exemptions.

# 2. CAMBODIA

**Main Authorities:** Accreditation Committee of Cambodia (ACC) / Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS)  
**Regulatory Strength:** Limited–Moderate  
**Overall Position:** Higher-education accreditation applies, and MoEYS has active LMS/digital-learning practices, but a detailed mandatory higher-education LMS feature standard was not confirmed.

## General QA Requirements

[ ] Verify accreditation/QA requirements for the ODL programme. — General QA

[ ] Define programme/course information and learning outcomes. — General QA

[ ] Maintain quality controls for learning materials. — General QA

[ ] Maintain appropriate assessment processes. — General QA

[ ] Provide learner support services. — General QA

## Official Digital-Learning Practice

The following are evident in official implementation or capacity-building activities but should **not automatically be treated as mandatory regulatory requirements**:

[ ] LMS use and management — Practice Only

[ ] Digital content creation and hosting — Practice Only

[ ] Multimedia/media use — Practice Only

[ ] Learner-instructor interaction — Practice Only

[ ] Interactive online learning activities — Practice Only

[ ] Internet/connectivity utilisation — Practice Only

[ ] Mobile/device access — Practice Only

[ ] Learning analytics/QA monitoring — Practice Only

[ ] Staff digital/LMS competence — Practice Only

[ ] Student ICT/digital-learning skills — Practice Only

### Items Requiring Regulatory Confirmation

Confirm directly with ACC/MoEYS whether the programme requires:

- LMS readiness evidence
- Self-learning materials
- Copyright/IP controls
- Learner-learner interaction
- Synchronous classes
- Asynchronous learning
- Instructor feedback
- Identity verification
- Anti-plagiarism systems
- Grades/progress access
- LMS induction
- IT helpdesk
- Digital library
- Special-needs accessibility
- Study-centre support
- LMS reliability monitoring
- Backup/disaster recovery
- Privacy/data protection
- Cybersecurity
- Performance tracking
- Formal learner feedback
- Cross-border/third-party controls

**Key Caution:** Official MoEYS use of LMS technology demonstrates recognised practice, but it should not be converted into a mandatory accreditation requirement without ACC/MoEYS confirmation.

[ ] Provide appropriate access to grades, progress and results.

## Learner Support

[ ] Maintain comprehensive learner-support services.

[ ] Provide IT helpdesk/technical support.

[ ] Provide digital library/e-learning resources.

[ ] Support students with disabilities/special needs.

[ ] Provide appropriate study-centre/regional support where applicable.

## Technology & Security

[ ] Ensure stable and adequate internet connectivity.

[ ] Monitor LMS capacity, reliability and performance.

[ ] Protect learner information and personal data.

[ ] Maintain an appropriately secure digital learning environment.

## Quality & Capability

[ ] Track learner engagement/performance.

[ ] Use analytics/monitoring for quality assurance.

[ ] Collect learner feedback and conduct course evaluation.

[ ] Ensure staff have adequate ODL/LMS competence.

[ ] Demonstrate LMS/platform readiness as part of accreditation.

[ ] Obtain appropriate ODL programme accreditation.

## Course Design & Content

[ ] Provide online access to digital learning content.

[ ] Develop appropriate self-instructional materials (SIM).

[ ] Maintain a dedicated process/unit for SIM development where required.

[ ] Review and quality-assure learning materials.

[ ] Use an appropriate multimedia/media mix.

[ ] Comply with copyright and intellectual-property requirements.

## Interaction & Pedagogy

[ ] Enable learner-instructor interaction.

[ ] Enable learner-learner interaction.

[ ] Provide synchronous/live interaction.

[ ] Provide asynchronous learning opportunities.

[ ] Include interactive online learning activities.

[ ] Ensure appropriate and timely instructor feedback.

## Assessment & Integrity

[ ] Maintain an appropriate ODL/online assessment framework.

[ ] Implement identity-verification measures where appropriate.

[ ] Maintain academic-integrity/anti-plagiarism controls.

[ ] Allow learners appropriate access to grades, progress and results.

## Learner Support

[ ] Provide student orientation/ODL induction.

[ ] Provide structured learner-support services.

[ ] Provide suitable IT/technical support.

[ ] Provide digital library/e-resource access.

[ ] Accommodate learners with disabilities or special requirements.

[ ] Provide learning-centre/study-centre support where applicable.

## Technology & Security

[ ] Ensure adequate internet/connectivity.

[ ] Monitor LMS capacity, reliability and system performance.

[ ] Protect student information and personal data.

[ ] Maintain a secure digital learning environment.

## Quality, Analytics & Capability

[ ] Track learner participation, engagement and performance.

[ ] Use LMS/programme data for QA monitoring.

[ ] Collect learner feedback and evaluate courses.

[ ] Ensure staff have appropriate ODL/LMS training and competence.

[ ] Prepare students for self-directed/online learning and relevant technology.

## External Arrangements

[ ] Review cross-border, outsourced and third-party arrangements as part of overall ODL quality assurance. — General QA

### Items Not Explicitly Confirmed in the Matrix

Specific explicit requirements were not confirmed for:

- Backup/redundancy/business continuity as a standalone LMS feature
- Mobile/device accessibility as a standalone feature

**Key Caution:** These should still be addressed as good system design and checked against other applicable Malaysian legislation, institutional cybersecurity requirements and programme-specific conditions.

[ ] Demonstrate LMS readiness/capability.

[ ] Ensure both institution and ODeL programme have appropriate NCHE accreditation.

[ ] Provide reliable access to digital learning content.

[ ] Develop appropriate Self-Learning Materials (SLMs).

[ ] Quality-assure and pre-test learning materials.

[ ] Use suitable multimedia/media for ODeL delivery.

[ ] Maintain copyright and intellectual-property controls.

## Interaction & Pedagogy

[ ] Enable learner-instructor interaction.

[ ] Enable learner-learner interaction.

[ ] Provide appropriate synchronous/live interaction.

[ ] Provide asynchronous learning.

[ ] Provide interactive learning activities.

[ ] Ensure instructor feedback is available.

## Assessment & Integrity

[ ] Maintain an appropriate ODeL assessment system.

[ ] Verify student identity and prevent impersonation.

[ ] Maintain plagiarism/academic-integrity controls.

[ ] Maintain comprehensive learner-support services.

[ ] Provide technical support/helpdesk services.

[ ] Provide digital library and electronic resources.

## Technology & Infrastructure

# MASTER LMS / ODL CHECKLIST

This can be used as the common baseline when developing an LMS intended to operate across all eight countries.

## A. Governance & Accreditation

[ ] Institution/provider has required approval.

[ ] ODL/online programme has required accreditation.

[ ] Dedicated LMS/online platform is available.

[ ] LMS readiness and capacity can be demonstrated to regulators.

[ ] Policies and procedures exist for LMS/ODL operation.

---

## B. Course Design & Content

[ ] Course information is clearly presented.

[ ] Learning outcomes are clearly stated.

[ ] Digital course materials are available online.

[ ] Self-learning materials are provided where required.

[ ] Learning materials undergo QA/review.

[ ] Multimedia/media mix is appropriate.

[ ] Copyright and intellectual-property requirements are addressed.

---

## C. Interaction & Learning

[ ] Student-instructor communication is supported.

[ ] Student-student interaction is supported.

[ ] Live/synchronous learning is supported where required.

[ ] Asynchronous learning is supported.

[ ] Interactive learning activities are available.

[ ] Instructors can provide feedback to learners.

---

## D. Assessment & Academic Integrity

[ ] Online assessment is supported.

[ ] Student identity can be verified where required.

[ ] Anti-plagiarism/academic-integrity controls exist.

[ ] Students can view grades/results/progress.

[ ] Assessment records are retained appropriately.

---

## E. Learner Support & Accessibility

[ ] Student orientation/LMS induction is provided.

[ ] Learner-support services are available.

[ ] Technical/IT helpdesk is available.

[ ] Digital library/e-resources are available.

[ ] Accessibility/special-needs requirements are supported.

[ ] Study-centre/regional support is available where required.

---

## F. Technology & Security

[ ] Internet/connectivity requirements are adequate.

[ ] LMS can handle expected numbers of users.

[ ] LMS availability and reliability are monitored.

[ ] Backup and recovery arrangements exist.

[ ] Privacy/data-protection controls exist.

[ ] Cybersecurity controls are implemented.

[ ] System works effectively across suitable devices.

---

## G. Quality & Analytics

[ ] Student engagement can be monitored.

[ ] Student performance can be monitored.

[ ] LMS data can support QA reporting.

[ ] Learner feedback can be collected.

[ ] Course evaluation and improvement processes exist.

---

## H. Staff & Student Capability

[ ] Academic staff receive LMS/ODL training.

[ ] Academic staff are competent to teach online.

[ ] Students receive appropriate ICT/LMS training.

[ ] Students understand how to study effectively through ODL.

---

## I. External / Cross-Border Arrangements

[ ] Outsourced LMS/services are governed through appropriate agreements.

[ ] Third-party providers meet institutional quality requirements.

[ ] Cross-border delivery complies with destination-country requirements.

[ ] External/partner study centres are appropriately controlled.
