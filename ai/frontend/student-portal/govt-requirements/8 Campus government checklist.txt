
# INTERNATIONAL QUALIFICATION RECOGNITION CHECKLIST

## 1. BOTSWANA

**Framework:** National Credit and Qualifications Framework (NCQF)  
**Levels:** 10  
**Credit System:** 1 credit = 10 notional learning hours  
**Main Authority:** Botswana Qualifications Authority (BQA)

### Qualification Reference
- Certificate I — Level 1 — 40 credits
- Certificate II — Level 2 — 40 credits
- Certificate III — Level 3 — 40 credits
- Certificate IV — Level 4 — 60 credits
- Certificate V — Level 5 — 120 credits
- Diploma — Level 6 — 240 credits
- Bachelor Degree — Level 7 — 360 credits
- Bachelor Honours Degree — Level 8 — 120 credits
- Postgraduate Certificate — Level 8 — 60 credits
- Postgraduate Diploma — Level 8 — 120 credits
- Master Degree — Level 9 — 240 credits
- Doctoral Degree — Level 10 — 360 credits

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Institution/provider is registered and accredited by BQA.  
[ ] Programme is accredited by BQA.  
[ ] Programme leads to a registered qualification.  
[ ] Qualification is registered on the NCQF.  
[ ] Qualification level and credit value are verified.  
[ ] Foreign awarding institution/programme is recognised in its home country.  
[ ] Professional body recognition is checked where applicable.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] Final qualification certificate  
[ ] Complete academic transcript  
[ ] Identity document/passport  
[ ] Evidence that awarding institution is recognised  
[ ] Evidence that programme is recognised/accredited  
[ ] Current BQA evaluation requirements and fees checked

**Important:** Do not recognise an award based only on its title or NCQF level.

## 2. CAMBODIA

**Framework:** Cambodia Qualifications Framework (CQF)  
**Levels:** 8  
**Main Authority:** Accreditation Committee of Cambodia (ACC)  
**Responsible Ministry:** Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS)

**Credit Reference:**
- 1 credit ≈ 15 lecture hours, or
- 30 laboratory/workshop hours, or
- 45 field/internship hours

### Qualification Reference
- Vocational Certificate — Level 1
- Technical & Vocational Certificate I — Level 2
- Technical & Vocational Certificate II — Level 3
- Technical & Vocational Certificate III — Level 4
- Higher Diploma / Associate Degree — Level 5
- Bachelor Degree — Level 6
- Master Degree — Level 7
- Doctorate — Level 8

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Institution is legally authorised/licensed.  
[ ] Institution's current ACC accreditation status is verified.  
[ ] Programme accreditation/status is verified with ACC.  
[ ] Any required MoEYS approval is verified.  
[ ] ACC status is identified as candidate, provisional or full where applicable.  
[ ] Qualification is mapped to the appropriate CQF level.  
[ ] Awarding authority is verified.  
[ ] Programme duration is verified.  
[ ] Credits and admission requirements are checked.  
[ ] Professional/sector approval is checked where required.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] Certified qualification certificate  
[ ] Complete academic transcript  
[ ] Passport/identity document  
[ ] Certified translation where required  
[ ] Authentication/legalisation where required  
[ ] Evidence that the qualification/institution is recognised in the country of origin  
[ ] Current case-specific requirements confirmed with ACC/MoEYS/receiving institution

**Important:** The English CQF document referenced in the workbook is a secondary source. Official ACC/MoEYS instruments should be used for formal decisions.

## 3. ESWATINI

**Framework:** Eswatini Qualifications Framework (ESQF)  
**Levels:** 10  
**Credit System:** Approximately 1 credit = 10 notional learning hours  
**Main Authorities:** Eswatini Qualifications Authority (EQA) and Eswatini Higher Education Council (ESHEC)

### Qualification Reference
- VET Certificate — Level 1 — 40 credits
- VET Certificate / Junior Certificate — Level 2 — 40 credits
- O-Level / EGCSE / IGCSE — Level 3 — 80 credits
- AS/A-Level/HIGCSE — Level 4 — 120 credits
- Diploma — Level 5 — 240 credits
- Associate Degree — Level 6 — 240 credits
- Bachelor Degree — Level 7 — 360 credits
- Bachelor Honours — Level 8 — 120 credits
- Professional Bachelor Degree — Level 8 — 480 credits
- Postgraduate Certificate — Level 8 — 120 credits
- Postgraduate Diploma — Level 8 — 120 credits
- Master Degree — Level 9 — 180 credits
- Doctoral Degree — Level 10 — 360 credits

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Institution's current ESHEC registration/accreditation is verified.  
[ ] Programme accreditation is verified.  
[ ] Qualification is registered on the ESQF before implementation.  
[ ] Qualification title is checked in the EQA official registry.  
[ ] ESQF registration number is verified.  
[ ] Qualification level is verified.  
[ ] Credit value is verified.  
[ ] Qualification registration status is current.  
[ ] Awarding/accrediting institution is verified.  
[ ] Professional council recognition is checked where applicable.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] Certified academic certificate(s)  
[ ] Academic transcript  
[ ] High-school exit qualification where required  
[ ] Proof of identity  
[ ] Birth certificate where required  
[ ] Current EQA application requirements checked  
[ ] Applicable evaluation fee checked

**Important:** Both programme accreditation and qualification registration must be checked.

- Junior/trade qualification — Level 2 — 120 credits

- Diploma — Level 6 — minimum 120 credits
- Bachelor Degree — Level 7 — minimum 360 credits
- Bachelor Honours / PG Certificate / PG Diploma — Level 8 — 120 credits
- Master Degree — Level 9 — minimum 120 credits
- Doctorate — Level 10 — 360 credits

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Institution is legally established/registered.  
[ ] Institution is recognised by CHE.  
[ ] Recognition covers the relevant campus/delivery site.  
[ ] Recognition was valid during the student's study period.  
[ ] Programme is accredited/approved by CHE.  
[ ] LQF level is confirmed.  
[ ] Credit volume is confirmed.  
[ ] Qualification type is confirmed.  
[ ] Awarding body is verified.  
[ ] Professional council recognition is checked where required.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] Certified final qualification certificate  
[ ] Complete academic transcript  
[ ] Identification document  
[ ] Translation where required  
[ ] Evidence that institution is recognised in country of origin  
[ ] Evidence that programme is recognised in country of origin  
[ ] Current CHE/MOET/receiving institution checklist obtained

**Important:** Confirm current LQF implementation and registration status for the specific qualification and period.

## 5. MALAYSIA

**Framework:** Malaysian Qualifications Framework (MQF), Second Edition 2024  
**Levels:** 8  
**Credit System:** 1 Malaysian credit = 40 notional learning hours  
**Main Authority:** Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA)  
**Responsible Ministry:** Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE)

### Qualification Reference
- Certificate — Level 1 — 15 credits
- Certificate — Level 2 — 30 credits
- Certificate — Level 3 — 60 credits
- Diploma — Level 4 — 90 credits
- Advanced Diploma — Level 5 — 40 credits
- Bachelor Degree — Level 6 — 120 credits
- Graduate Certificate — Level 6 — 34 credits
- Graduate Diploma — Level 6 — 64 credits
- Postgraduate Certificate — Level 7 — 20 credits
- Postgraduate Diploma — Level 7 — 30 credits
- Master Degree (Coursework/Mixed Mode) — Level 7 — 40 credits
- Doctoral Degree (Coursework/Mixed Mode) — Level 8 — 80 credits

**Note:** Research-mode Master's and Doctoral qualifications do not have a fixed credit rating in the MQF table referenced.

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Higher education provider is lawfully established/approved.  
[ ] Institutional/self-accreditation status is checked where relevant.  
[ ] Programme accreditation status is verified.  
[ ] Provisional Accreditation is distinguished from Full Accreditation.  
[ ] Full Accreditation is verified in the Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR).  
[ ] MQF qualification level is confirmed.  
[ ] Qualification type is confirmed.  
[ ] Minimum credit requirement is met.  
[ ] Professional programme recognition is checked separately where applicable.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] Qualification certificate  
[ ] Complete academic transcript  
[ ] Identification document  
[ ] Supporting evidence for equivalency  
[ ] Competent authority/embassy attestation where required  
[ ] Translation if documents are not in Bahasa Malaysia or English  
[ ] MQA entry qualification or tertiary qualification equivalency assessment obtained where required

**Important:** MQF level comparison alone does not prove programme accreditation, professional recognition or suitability for admission/employment.

## 6. NAMIBIA

**Framework:** Namibia National Qualifications Framework (NQF)  
**Levels:** 10  
**Credit System:** 1 credit = 10 notional learning hours  
**Main Authorities:** Namibia Qualifications Authority (NQA) and National Council for Higher Education (NCHE)

### Qualification Reference
- Certificate — Levels 1–8 — minimum 40 credits
- Diploma — Levels 5–8 — minimum 120 credits
- Bachelor Degree — Level 7 — minimum 360 credits
- Bachelor Honours Degree — Level 8 — additional 120 credits
- Professional Bachelor Degree — Level 8 — minimum 480 credits
- Master Degree — Level 9 — minimum 240 credits
- Doctoral Degree — Level 10 — minimum 360 credits

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Private higher education institution is registered with the appropriate NCHE arrangements.  
[ ] Institution/provider has the required NQA accreditation.  
[ ] Programme accreditation scope is verified.  
[ ] Accreditation expiry/validity period is checked.  
[ ] Qualification is registered on the NQF.  
[ ] Qualification type is verified.  
[ ] NQF level is verified.  
[ ] Credit distribution is verified.  
[ ] Professional registration is checked for regulated occupations.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] NQA qualification evaluation application  
[ ] Certified qualification certificate(s)  
[ ] Complete transcript/academic records  
[ ] Identity document  
[ ] Translation where applicable  
[ ] Authentication where applicable  
[ ] Verification from awarding institution/authority where required

**Important:** For private higher education institutions, NCHE registration and NQA accreditation are separate requirements. Check both.

## 7. SIERRA LEONE

**Framework Status:** Comprehensive national NQF is still under development according to the workbook.  
**2021 Framework:** National Qualifications Framework for Tertiary Education is treated as a sector/project resource rather than a fully enacted comprehensive national framework.  
**Main Authorities:** Tertiary Education Commission (TEC), Ministry of Technical and Higher Education (MTHE), and NCTVE for relevant TVET matters.

### Qualification Reference
Do NOT automatically assign a national NQF level to:
- Certificate
- Diploma
- Bachelor Degree
- Master Degree
- Doctorate

Instead, use the individual programme and qualification documentation.

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Institution is registered/accredited by TEC.  
[ ] Institution is registered in the correct institutional category.  
[ ] Institution has lawful awarding authority.  
[ ] Affiliation arrangements are verified where applicable.  
[ ] Programme is accredited/approved by TEC.  
[ ] Delivery location is confirmed.  
[ ] Accreditation/approval was valid during the award period.  
[ ] Certificate/diploma/TVET arrangements are checked with the relevant NCTVE authority where applicable.  
[ ] Award certificate is verified.  
[ ] Academic transcript is verified.  
[ ] Awarding/affiliation authority is verified.  
[ ] Professional/statutory recognition is checked for regulated occupations.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] Certified qualification certificate(s)  
[ ] Complete academic transcript  
[ ] Identity document  
[ ] Authentication evidence  
[ ] Evidence of recognition in the country of origin  
[ ] Written case-specific requirements obtained from the receiving authority

**Important:** Do not treat the 2021 tertiary framework as a fully enacted comprehensive national NQF unless current legislation confirms this.

## 8. UGANDA

**Framework:** Uganda Higher Education Qualifications Framework (UHEQF), 2026  
**National Architecture:** 9 levels; higher education primarily Levels 4–9  
**Credit Reference:** 1 credit = approximately 10 notional learning hours, subject to confirmation against the current NCHE framework  
**Main Authority:** National Council for Higher Education (NCHE)

### Qualification Reference
- Higher Education Certificate/Foundation — Level 4 — approximately 120 credits
- Ordinary Diploma — Level 5 — approximately 240 credits
- Advanced/Higher Diploma — Level 6
- Bachelor Degree — Level 7 — approximately 360 / 480 / 600 credits depending on programme
- Master Degree / Postgraduate qualifications — Level 8
- Master Degree — approximately 240–300 credits
- Doctorate — Level 9 — approximately 540 credits

### Recognition Checklist
[ ] Institution has current NCHE licence/registration.  
[ ] Institutional classification is verified.  
[ ] University charter/lawful status is checked where applicable.  
[ ] Current NCHE list is checked for changes or revocations.  
[ ] Programme has NCHE accreditation/approval.  
[ ] Correct campus is verified.  
[ ] Delivery mode is verified.  
[ ] Programme approval/accreditation period is verified.  
[ ] Qualification is mapped to the current UHEQF.  
[ ] Credits are verified.  
[ ] Learning outcomes are considered.  
[ ] Institution/programme recognition was valid during the study period.  
[ ] Professional council recognition is checked where applicable.

### Foreign Qualification Documents
[ ] NCHE/REQIMS application  
[ ] Certified qualification certificate(s)  
[ ] Certified academic transcript(s)  
[ ] Passport/identity document  
[ ] Proof of name change where applicable  
[ ] English translation for non-English documents  
[ ] Required evaluation fee paid  
[ ] Awarding institution verification provided where requested by NCHE

**Important:** Use the current 2026 NCHE framework for decisions. Do not rely on the older 2016 framework where newer requirements apply.

# GENERAL CHECKLIST FOR ALL COUNTRIES

For every international qualification being assessed:

[ ] Verify the institution is legally recognised.  
[ ] Verify the institution's accreditation status during the period of study.  
[ ] Verify the exact campus/delivery location where applicable.  
[ ] Verify the programme was accredited/approved.  
[ ] Verify programme accreditation was valid when the student studied.  
[ ] Verify the awarding institution/body.  
[ ] Verify qualification title.  
[ ] Verify qualification framework level where applicable.  
[ ] Verify minimum credits/learning volume where applicable.  
[ ] Verify the academic transcript.  
[ ] Verify admission requirements and programme duration where relevant.  
[ ] Authenticate the qualification certificate.  
[ ] Obtain foreign qualification evaluation/equivalency where required.  
[ ] Verify professional/statutory recognition for regulated programmes.  
[ ] Record the date on which the recognition check was completed.  
[ ] Keep supporting evidence/screenshots/official authority confirmation with the assessment record.
