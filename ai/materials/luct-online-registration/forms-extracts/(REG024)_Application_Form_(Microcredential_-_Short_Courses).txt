Application Submission: The submission of this application form does not guarantee admission to the course. Admission decisions will be based on the applicant's qualifications and available spaces in the selected course.
Documentation: The applicant has to provide all necessary documentation and information requested by the university to support their application, including academic transcripts, certificates, and any other relevant documents.
Course Fees: Enrolment in the short course is subject to the payment of applicable fees. Details of the course fees, including payment deadlines and methods, will be provided upon admission.
