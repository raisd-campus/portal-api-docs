Operator help · 20 October 2026 · local implementation

University Flows: Admissions

Configure supported campus rules in Control. Published versions apply to new applications; existing cases retain their policy. This owner-approved implementation supersedes the earlier hard-coded-first hold.

Configure and publish

  1. Activate Control with flows.configure; open University Flows → Admissions and choose a campus.
  2. Edit supported settings, dates and reviewers using the shared controls.
  3. Save draft with an effective UTC date and change reason.
  4. Validate/preview local/international and new/returning cases, including manual decisions and skipped stages.
  5. Publish an immutable version. Restore older settings by publishing another version.

Starting policy and manual decisions

Eligibility precedes Registration Fee collection. Application Fee and admissions Registration Fee are the same charge per programme application. New international students settle first-year tuition or obtain prior approval for semester payment. Each issued Offer needs its own verified signed copy. English is due one calendar year after recorded arrival. IO reviews medical matters; SSD manages arrival/departure. Module registration initially needs manual Registry clearance, Bursary and Faculty review, then CAF.

All eight campuses start with this owner-selected Demo template. Missing prices/dates, local or returning-student rules and fee credits remain manual. Disabling collection never waives debts. Late exceptions bind to their specific deadline/document/quote.

Access and storage stay separate

Flow settings never grant portal access. Assign IO/SSD or other review desks explicitly to an existing campus Admin in Control People. Applicant access remains fixed. Policy configuration persists in the configured PostgreSQL database or the private local policy file; application and evidence storage retain their independent modes.

Later flows

Priorities: intake/late entry and deferment, reminders/escalations, scholarship/waiver approvals, transfers, Add/Drop, returning registration, graduation and transcript holds. Each needs an approved scope through the same framework; no arbitrary scripts or automatic tax/refund formulas.

Full operator, policy, storage and verification guide. This source delivery does not claim hosted deployment or Live CMS acceptance.
