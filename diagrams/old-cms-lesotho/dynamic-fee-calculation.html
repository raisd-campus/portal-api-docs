Standup 9 Oct · Dynamic fee proposal · 10 Oct 2026

Dynamic fee calculation

Proposed approach: configurable rules (campus profile + C1–C10) instead of copying CMS hard-coded SP logic. Canonical Markdown: dynamic-fee-calculation.md. Seeds: campus-finance-profiles.yaml. Faid checklist: campus-finance-profile.html.

Proposed — for review. Meeting principle: use configurable rules, do not reproduce hard-coded legacy logic. Dexter validates C1–C10 against SPs before final config; Faid Accepts seeds.
1. WhyProblem 2. ArchitectureProfile → engine 3. Rule orderC1–C10 4. Waiting onDexter · Faid

1. Why dynamic

CMS procedures hard-code currency stamps (M/R/MYR), semester vs modular branches, and some repeat amounts. Raisd should evaluate a campus finance profile so Finance can change applies / amount / currency without an engineering release for every campus tweak.

2. Architecture (sketch)

flowchart TD
  P[campus_finance_profiles.yaml] --> E[Fee engine Portal API]
  E --> A[Applicant fee step Plane A]
  E --> S[Student projection Plane C]
  E --> W[CAP-53 W1 proof Plane B]
  W --> CMS[CMS SPs Live SoR]

3C3/C4Semester and/or modular mode
4C5Repeat/ADD add-ons (overrides listed, not baked in)
